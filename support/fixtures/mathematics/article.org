Equations belong in the reading flow. The indexed value \(x_i\) and the fraction \(\frac{1}{2}\) remain mathematics, without turning underscores into emphasis.

* A running total

For a sequence of observations, the arithmetic mean is

\[
\bar{x} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i.
\]

Inline dollar notation also works: $a_b + c^2$.

* Working through a system

The aligned steps retain their relationships and spacing.

\begin{align*}
2x + y &= 7 \\
x - y &= 2 \\
3x &= 9
\end{align*}

The same system can be written as a matrix equation.

\begin{equation*}
\begin{bmatrix}2 & 1 \\ 1 & -1\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}x \\ y\end{bmatrix}
= \begin{bmatrix}7 \\ 2\end{bmatrix}
\end{equation*}

* Wide equations

On a narrow screen, the equation scrolls within its own region; the prose should never force the entire page sideways.

\[
\mathcal{L}(\theta) = \sum_{i=1}^{n}\log p(x_i \mid \theta) + \lambda_1\sum_{j=1}^{m}\theta_j^2 + \lambda_2\sum_{j=1}^{m}\lvert\theta_j\rvert + \lambda_3\sum_{j=1}^{m}(\theta_j-\theta_{j-1})^2
\]
